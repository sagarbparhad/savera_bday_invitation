A little letter

Ten years of you

Ten years ago you arrived — tiny fists, enormous eyes — and quietly rearranged all of our lives around your laugh.

Since then you have filled our home with glitter in places glitter should never be, songs at breakfast, drawings on every wall (we kept every single one), and a kind of love we didn't know existed before you.

You are brave in small ways and kind in loud ones. You dance before the music starts. You make strangers smile at bus stops.

Ten looks so good on you already. Come celebrate the sparkle.
